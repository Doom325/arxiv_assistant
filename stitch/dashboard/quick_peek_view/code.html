Analysis of Emergent Reasoning

calendar_today March 14, 2024
schedule 12 Min Read

Abstract

The paper investigates the phenomenon of "reasoning" in large-scale language models, questioning whether observed logical steps are truly emergent or simply sophisticated pattern matching. Through a series of novel benchmarks focused on non-linear deductive tasks, the authors demonstrate that scale alone does not guarantee semantic coherence, yet specific architectural bottlenecks appear to facilitate higher-order logic synthesis...
